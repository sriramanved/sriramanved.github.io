I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Columbia University, advised by Adam Block.

My research studies the foundations of learning and decision-making, with a current focus on language-model post-training, imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and inference-time algorithms.

I am particularly interested in understanding how learning algorithms behave when the teacher, reward, or model class is imperfect—and in turning those insights into practical methods for training and improving language models.
