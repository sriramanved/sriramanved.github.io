Hi, I'm Ved, a second-year Computer Science PhD student at Columbia University, where I am part of the Theory Group and affiliated with the Columbia Core AI Lab (CAIL). I am fortunate to be advised by Adam Block, and I work closely with Max Dabagia.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning and its applications to language models. I aim to use theory and empirics in tandem to design algorithmic interventions with provable guarantees. I’m currently working on the theoretical underpinnings of on-policy distillation, disentangling the different factors that make this popular post-training technique so effective in practice.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Cornell University, where I worked with Karthik Sridharan and Sainyam Galhotra on theoretical machine learning.

Feel free to reach out at ved at cs dot columbia dot edu.
